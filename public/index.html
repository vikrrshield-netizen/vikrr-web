VIKRR Asset Shield

Built for maintenance, production and audit pressure.

Asset Shield is designed around real plant workflows: a fault appears in the kiosk, maintenance receives the task, the asset card keeps the history and reports provide the evidence.

What it replaces

Paper checklists, disconnected spreadsheets, undocumented repairs, missing asset history and unclear responsibility between production, maintenance and quality.

Asset cardsEvery room, machine and device has status, relationships, documents, tasks and work history.
Inspection evidenceBuilding inspections are closed as dated records with defects, tasks and XLSX/PDF exports.
Production contextMachines, recipes, batches, dataloggers and gearbox readings connect maintenance to actual operations.
Operator kioskFast role-based actions for shop-floor reporting without exposing admin complexity.
Audit-readyTimestamped records, responsible users, exports and history for compliance review.
Mobile-firstDesigned for phones, kiosks and production-floor usage, not only office desktops.
Secure by designRole-based permissions, protected PIN login, Firestore rules and controlled storage access.
Domain-ledBuilt from food production, maintenance and quality workflows rather than generic templates.
