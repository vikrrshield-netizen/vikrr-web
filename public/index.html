VIKRR Asset Shield

Built for assets, work orders and evidence.

Asset Shield is designed around operational workflows: an issue appears, the responsible team gets the task, the asset card keeps the history and reports provide the evidence.

What it replaces

Paper checklists, disconnected spreadsheets, undocumented work, missing asset history and unclear responsibility between teams.

Asset cardsEvery room, machine and device has status, relationships, documents, tasks and work history.
Inspection evidenceChecks are closed as dated records with findings, tasks and XLSX/PDF exports.
Operational contextMachines, sensors, documents and task history connect daily work to reliable records.
Operator kioskFast role-based actions for shop-floor reporting without exposing admin complexity.
01 Core module

Asset cards keep the full equipment story.
